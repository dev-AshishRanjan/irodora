# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone working on Irodora — maintainers, contractors, reviewers, and AI agents acting
on our behalf — is expected to be respectful, direct, and honest.

**Expected:** clear communication, good-faith disagreement, accepting correction, giving
credit, and respecting the time and attention of others.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, discriminatory language or conduct, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

## Two standards specific to this product

**Honesty about accuracy.** Irodora makes claims about physical colour. Overstating what a
consumer camera can measure, presenting an estimate as a measurement, or reporting a
verification you did not run is a conduct issue here, not merely a technical one. If you
did not run the gate, say so.

**Respect for the culture we draw on.** The product is built on Japanese colour tradition.
Treat that material with the care it deserves: cite sources, distinguish historical fact
from contemporary interpretation, do not flatten centuries of practice into decoration,
and do not reach for stereotype when the real material is richer.

## Accessibility is not optional

We build for people with colour-vision deficiency. Arguing that an accessibility
requirement is "just a nice-to-have" is arguing against the product's purpose.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@irodora.com**. Reports are handled confidentially. Project
maintainers will investigate and respond, and may remove access, revert contributions, or
end an engagement in response to conduct they judge inappropriate.

Maintainers who do not uphold this standard are subject to it themselves.
