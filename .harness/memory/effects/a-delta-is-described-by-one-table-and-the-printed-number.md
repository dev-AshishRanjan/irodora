# E-175 — A delta is described by one table, and by the printed number

**Link:** `bands.ts#DELTA_E00_BANDS` → `delta-readings.ts`, the catalogues, 04 (F-245), 08 (F-249),
the inventories **Guard:** the band-edge golden set; the typed key maps and the no-match test
**Severity:** high **Feature:** F-222

## Why B changes when A does

Every word beside a ΔE00 or one of its components comes from ADR-0125's four bands. A total is banded
as it is PRINTED, and a component on its weighted term, which is in ΔE00's unit. Moving an edge moves
04's headline, 08's readings and every later instrument together, which is why the edges are golden
data quoting their sources.

## What the edges rest on

0.8 and 1.8 are the 50:50 % perceptibility and acceptability thresholds the 2019 review reports in
ΔE00, measured on dental ceramic. 5 is the top of CIEDE2000's range. So a band describes the number on
the screen, never what a person will see: the ADR says so, and it is the honest limit of the source.
